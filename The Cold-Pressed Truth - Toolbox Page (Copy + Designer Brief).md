## Part 2 — Designer Brief

**Two deliverables from one build:** a grayscale version for the book interior, and a color
version for the website in the CPG Founders Group brand (warm paper + deep navy + gold).

Design in color first, then convert — but check the grayscale constantly, because the B&W
version is the one that has to survive ink on uncoated paper.

### The graphic: the toolbox bundle

A single hero image showing the whole kit at once. It needs to read as *volume* — "look how
much is in here" — in about one second.

Show six things, overlapping and slightly angled, one asset in front:

1. **The book** — *The Cold-Pressed Truth*, Option B cover (green vegetable letterforms).
   Files: `public/images/book-covers/cover-b.jpg` in the repo.
2. **Babu AI** — a phone or laptop screen showing the Babu chat interface. This one should sit
   forward in the stack. It's the headline item.
3. **The WhatsApp group** — a phone screen showing an active group thread. Blur or fake the
   message text; do not show real member names or numbers.
4. **A spreadsheet** — one of the calculators open, ideally with a visible chart so it reads as
   a real financial model, not a blank grid.
5. **A workshop still** — Jeff at the whiteboard, framed as a video player. Use
   `public/images/jeff-teaching.webp`.
6. **The white paper** — *Fatal Flaws*, cover visible with the title legible.

**Rule:** if a title isn't legible at 2 inches tall, don't include that asset. Better five
readable pieces than eight muddy ones.

**No** mockup chrome with glass glare, reflections, or hard drop shadows. They look fine on a
monitor and turn to sludge in print.

### Version A — Book interior (grayscale)

- **Trim:** 6×9 (confirm with the printer). Nothing within 0.5" of the trim; watch the gutter
  on the inside edge.
- **Layout, top to bottom:** headline → 2-line intro → hero image → "Start with these two" →
  the rest of the list → URL + QR at the foot.
- **Grayscale only**, converted before you design so you're not guessing at the result.
- **300 DPI minimum** for the image; **600–1200 DPI** for the QR and any line art.
- **Body text 100% black.** No gray text — 60% black on uncoated interior stock reads muddy.
- **20% minimum tonal separation** between any two adjacent grays. Two shades that look distinct
  on screen will merge on press.
- **No gradients, no drop shadows, no glows.** They band and gray out. Use hard edges and white
  space to separate elements.
- **Cap solid blacks around 85–90%**, not a 100% flood — big black blocks show through the page.
- The Option B cover is green-on-cream. In grayscale the letterforms lose contrast fast. Boost
  the contrast on that asset specifically so "COLD PRESSED TRUTH" still reads.

### Version B — Website (color) — CPG Founders Group brand

Goes on **cpgfoundersgroup.com/toolbox**, in the hero beside the opt-in form and/or above the
"Start with these two" cards.

**Build this in the CPG Founders Group identity, not a one-off palette.** In the brand system,
CPG Founders Group is a **human surface: warm paper + deep navy + gold.** Signal blue belongs to
Babu (machine surfaces) and appears here only on the Babu asset itself.

**Core palette — use these exact values:**

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary dark / type | Deep navy | `#0B1A2E` |
| Secondary surface | Ridge blue | `#1B3A5F` |
| Machine / Babu only | Signal blue | `#2B4CF0` |
| Emphasis on navy | Summit gold | `#DFA13C` |
| Gold on light surfaces | Deep amber | `#A56A16` |
| Background | Warm paper | `#F7F5F1` |
| Muted / secondary | Stone | `#75828F` |

**Usage ratio: 70 / 20 / 10.** 70% neutral foundation (paper, navy, stone), 20% brand
temperature (ridge blue, deep amber), 10% signal or emphasis (summit gold). Do not let gold
creep past roughly a tenth of the composition — it stops being emphasis the moment it's
everywhere.

**The one rule that will bite you:** Summit gold `#DFA13C` only clears 2.3:1 on a light
background. It is a *navy-surface* color (7.7:1 on deep navy, excellent). On paper or white,
use Deep amber `#A56A16` instead. Never set gold type on paper.

**The mark:** the CPG Founders Group mountain — gold foothill, deep navy summit, ridge blue
shoulder, signal blue summit flag. Download the vector directly:
`https://cpgfoundersgroup.com/images/brand/cpg-founders-mark.svg`

**Specs:**
- The `/toolbox` hero sits on **deep navy `#0B1A2E`**, so build the color version to work on
  dark. Transparent background with light edges, or a soft summit-gold glow behind the stack
  for separation.
- **2400px wide**, transparent PNG, plus a WebP export. Site images run with Next.js
  optimization off, so **keep the WebP under 300KB** or the page gets slow.
- Also supply a **1200×630 version on a solid background** for the OG / social share card.

### The QR code (print only)

- **1" × 1" minimum** printed. Pure black on pure white.
- **Error correction level H** so a scuff or a bad print run doesn't kill it.
- **4-module quiet zone** all around. No logo in the center, no rounded "designer" dots, no color.
- Encodes exactly: `https://cpgfoundersgroup.com/toolbox` — nothing appended. No UTM string.
- Delivered as **vector (EPS or SVG)**, never a raster.
- **Test the printed proof with three different phones** before it goes to press.

### Type (print)

- Headline: the display face used on chapter openers, all caps, centered, roughly 20–24pt.
- Body and list: text serif, ~11pt, generous leading.
- **Bold the kit name, roman for what's in it.** That contrast is what makes the list skimmable
  in a single glance — it's the most important typographic decision on the page.
- URL: bold, a step larger than body. It's the second-most important element after the headline.

### Deliverables checklist

1. Print page as **PDF/X-1a**, grayscale, fonts embedded, 0.125" bleed if the image bleeds.
2. Hero image, grayscale, **300 DPI TIFF** + layered PSD.
3. Hero image, color, **2400px transparent PNG** + WebP under 300KB.
4. OG card, **1200×630**, solid background.
5. QR code as **vector**.
