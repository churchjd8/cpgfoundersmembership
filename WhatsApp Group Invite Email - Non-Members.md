# WhatsApp Group Invite Email

**Audience:** Everyone on Jeff's list who is NOT yet in the CPG Founders WhatsApp group
**Goal:** Get them in the group. Free, no pitch.
**Link:** https://cpgfoundersgroup.com/founders-only

---

## Subject line options

1. we just crossed 200 founders
2. 200 founders in the group chat
3. come join the founders group with 200 others

---

## Body

Hi {{first_name}},

We just crossed 200 founders in the CPG Founders WhatsApp group!

When I started it I figured maybe a few dozen people would want a place to ask the questions you can't really ask anywhere else. The ones you don't want to put in front of your board, or your investors, or your own team.

Turns out a lot of us have those questions.

Here's what came through in that group in the last week alone.

One founder walked everybody through an AI inventory management system they built themselves. Screen recording, the whole thing, just handed it over.

Another one needed a military broker and had a warm referral within a few hours.

Somebody shared the merchandising app they actually use every day, not the one with the best sales deck.

And one founder got connected to a pre-seed angel group.

That's one week, nobody got paid for any of it, and nobody was selling anything.

This is the part I love most at this stage of my career. I spent a lot of years learning things the hard way and paying full tuition for it, so watching founders cut each other's learning curves down in a group chat is about as good as it gets.

If you're not in there yet, come join us. It's free and it stays free.

[Join the CPG Founders Group](https://cpgfoundersgroup.com/founders-only)

Come say hi when you get in, tell us what you're building and where you're stuck right now. Somebody in there has already been through it!

Jeff

## Notes

- Same framing as the LinkedIn post, adapted for email. If both go out, space them a few days apart so the list does not read the same story twice in a row.
- The milestone opener works whether or not they are already in the group, so no need for an "ignore this if you're already in" line.
- Verify the real member count before sending. If it is well past 200, use the real number.
- Keep the four examples specific. The specificity is the whole pitch.
- Refresh the examples every time this gets resent. Same structure, new week.
- If sending from Kajabi, still suppress anyone tagged as a group member where we do have the match.
