# CPG Match - Vendor Review Email (Jeff to List)

Adapted from Nina's draft, rewritten in Jeff's voice for the Kajabi list. Review and waitlist both live at cpgmatch.com (two buttons on the same page).

**Subject:** "Who's the best vendor for...?"

**Alt subjects:**
- Help me build the vendor list founders keep asking for
- The question I get asked most

**Preview text:** Takes 3-5 minutes, can be anonymous, and you get early access.

---

Hi {{first_name}},

"Who's the best vendor for this?"

It might be the question I get asked most. Across our groups, newsletters, events and programs, we hear from thousands of founders and operators every year, and this one comes up again and again. Who should I use for packaging design? Which copacker? Is this broker any good? Who can actually help us on Amazon?

Building a CPG brand takes a village, and finding the right partners at the right time and the right price is high stakes. I've watched founders pick a vendor off a great sales pitch and lose months (and a lot of cash) before they realized it was the wrong fit. The right partner can change the trajectory of a business. The wrong one becomes very expensive tuition.

So we're building CPG Match, a directory of CPG vendors powered by reviews from founders and operators who have actually hired them, paid the invoice, and lived with the results.

But first, I need your help.

If you've worked with a vendor, good or bad, please take 3-5 minutes and review them here:

**Review a vendor: https://cpgmatch.com**

A few things to know:

- Any vendor counts: formulation, fractional CFOs, copackers, packaging, brokers, packaging designers, ecommerce, and more.
- We're launching packaging design and ecommerce support (Amazon, online ads, Instacart, etc.) first, so those reviews are especially helpful right now.
- We verify every reviewer is a founder or operator, but you can keep your review anonymous.
- Anyone who submits a review gets early access to the directory.

The great vendors deserve the credit. One of the most meaningful things you can do for a partner who took care of you is help more brands find them.

And the ones you'd warn people about? Those reviews matter just as much. I'm sorry if you went through it, because a bad vendor relationship is a miserable experience. But your warning could be make or break for a founder who is about to bet their time, their dollars, and their shot at success on that same vendor.

Thank you for helping build this. It's the resource I wish every founder had.

Jeff

P.S. Not ready to write a review yet? Join the waitlist on the same page and we'll let you know the moment the directory launches: https://cpgmatch.com
