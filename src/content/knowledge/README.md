## The Book (canonical)

**"The Cold-Pressed Truth: A Founder's Field Guide to Building, Scaling, and Selling a Consumer Brand"** by Jeff Church · Foreword by Jay Shetty · ~53,000 words · 23 chapters in three parts + epilogue and appendix.
Source docs live in the repo root: `8.2.26 Cold-Pressed Truth Manuscript .docx` and `The Cold-Pressed Truth - 2 Page Summary Brief 8.2.26.docx`.

- **[cpt-00-book-facts.md](cpt-00-book-facts.md)** — Title block, jacket copy, advance praise, Jay Shetty foreword pull-quotes, credential lines, TOC, funnel assets, and a **pre-print flag list** (inconsistencies to resolve before cover/marketing copy locks). Use this for anything cover-, jacket-, pitch-, or campaign-related.
- **[cpt-part1-foundation.md](cpt-part1-foundation.md)** — Part I, Ch. 1–9. Founder's DNA, the end-zone story, pre-launch prep, the $250K roadmap, M.A.P. Framework, Stage-Gate, Life Stages of a Brand, fundraising, culture, ten misconceptions.
- **[cpt-part2-scaling.md](cpt-part2-scaling.md)** — Part II, Ch. 10–17. Scaling without losing control, systems and margin, winning the shelf, the art of the pivot, the Coca-Cola partnership, retail relationships, Coke's fateful decision, the wellness-shot pivot.
- **[cpt-part3-exit.md](cpt-part3-exit.md)** — Part III, Ch. 18–23 + Epilogue + Appendix A. The turnaround and sale, the Good/Bad/Ugly, Babu's 15 rules, 17 fatal flaws, 10 universal principles, the climb, ringing the bell, retail metrics glossary, acknowledgements.

## How to Use

1. Pull the story, quote, or stat from **storybank.md**.
2. Go to the relevant `cpt-part*.md` only if you need the surrounding context or exact wording.
3. Cross-reference **`jeff_voice_profile.md`** (repo root) for voice and style before drafting.
4. For book cover, jacket, blurb, or launch-campaign work, read **cpt-00-book-facts.md** first — including the pre-print flags.
