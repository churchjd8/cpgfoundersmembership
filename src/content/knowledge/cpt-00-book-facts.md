## The One-Line Version

An eight-time founder tells the unvarnished story of building Suja Juice from an ice closet to $100 million in six years — and of the July 3rd phone call when Coca-Cola declined to buy the rest of it — and turns three decades of wins and wipeouts into a working manual for anyone building a consumer brand.

## The Persona: "Babu"

Swahili for grandpa. Earned on Kilimanjaro (climbing with his kids, ~day three, thin oxygen) — given by the local guides partly for his slow pace, partly because he kept offering unsolicited advice on the trail. His grandkids call him Babu now.

> "Somewhere between the mountain and the boardroom, I realized that's exactly the role I've always played: the experienced guy walking alongside you, pointing out what lies ahead while honestly assessing the truth of what's behind."

**Posture:** deliberately anti-hype. A memoir that keeps stopping to hand you a tool; an operating manual that keeps admitting what it cost. Failures get as much airtime as wins.

**Closing image (Ch. 23):** "Someone who walks ahead, pointing out the cliffs, the switchbacks, and the places where the view is worth the climb."

## Who It's For

CPG/consumer founders first, but the frameworks are written to travel. Also operators, early employees, and investors who want an honest read on what the inside of a fast-scaling brand actually feels like.

## ⚠️ Pre-Print Flags (found on read-through — decide before cover/marketing copy locks)

These matter because marketing copy will inherit whichever number we pick.

1. **Employee count is inconsistent.** Ch. 1 says "300+"; Ch. 19 says "nearly 350 employees at the height"; Epilogue says "400 families"; the 2-page summary says 280. Pick one canonical number for all marketing.
2. **"New York Times bestselling author"** appears in the About the Author section. Unsupported for a debut — remove or substantiate.
3. **Jeff's age flips inside the Epilogue** — "at sixty-four" then "at sixty-five, I'm still a startup junkie." Ch. 19 says 64.
4. **Launch store count:** Ch. 6 says 50 Whole Foods stores (SoCal); Ch. 11 says "thirty-five Whole Foods stores in 2012."
5. **Launch shelf life:** 22 days (Ch. 6, Ch. 15) vs. 24 days (Ch. 6, Ch. 11).
6. **Name spellings:** Scott Uzell (Ch. 16) vs. Scott Uzzell (Ch. 14) — correct is **Uzzell**. Jason Polinsky (Ch. 12) vs. Jason Polinski (Ch. 16). Bill Wieland (Ch. 15) vs. Bill Weiland (Ch. 19).
7. **TOC vs. body chapter-title mismatches:** Ch. 4 ("Industry and Segment" vs. "Category"), Ch. 11 ("Scale Through Systems" vs. "Building an Engine That Lasts"), Ch. 12 ("The Battle of the Shelf" vs. "Winning the Shelf"), Ch. 13 ("Innovation and the Art of the Pivot" vs. "The Art of the Pivot"), Ch. 19 ("Personal Reflections" vs. body's "The Good, the Bad, and the Ugly" framing).
8. **Formatting break:** the Part III heading and the Chapter 18 heading are merged into one line ("PART III: The Exit and Key LearningsChapter 18: The Moment of Truth").
9. **Stage numbering conflict:** Ch. 10 opens "After Stage 1 ... now comes the challenges of Stage 2: scaling quickly without losing control" — but Ch. 6's four-stage model puts scaling at Stage 3 (Stage 2 = Proof of Concept).
10. **Repeat-rate threshold conflict:** Ch. 2 says dig in if below 10% (DTC/Amazon context); Ch. 6 says below 20% means you have churn, not proof of concept (retail context). Reconcile or label the contexts explicitly.
11. **The "ice closet"** — the Epilogue's origin image ("the ice closet of my partner's nightclub") never appears earlier in the book. It's one of the strongest visuals in the manuscript and is currently unearned. Strong candidate to seed into the Preface or Ch. 6.
12. Suja capital raised: $110M in first seven years (Ch. 7, Ch. 14) vs. $115M over eight years (Ch. 16). Reconcilable, but pick one for press.
13. Ch. 6: "roughly 7% make it to Phase 2, leaving nearly 4,700 behind" — 7% of 5,000 leaves 4,650.
14. Ch. 13 says Suja grew "to over $300 million in revenue in its first decade," which sits oddly against the ~$100–120M figures during Jeff's tenure. Clarify it's post-tenure.
