## Hot 3 — Philosophy
**Subject:** revenue without margin is ego

{{first_name}},

Most founders chase doors. More retailers, more SKUs, more revenue to show investors.

It's a mistake, because revenue at a bad margin just burns cash faster, and it's how good brands die with great top lines.

Think about it like this instead: margin first, velocity second, doors third. Prove the product sells at a price that makes money in the doors you have. Then expand.

It's slower for about a year. Then it's the reason you're still here.

If you're about to add doors and you're not sure the math holds, we should talk.

## Hot 4 — Plan
**Subject:** my 3-step margin rebuild

{{first_name}},

When a founder brings me a margin problem, here's the order I work it:

1. **Net price by channel.** What you actually keep per unit after every deduction, in every channel. Most founders have never seen this number.
2. **Pack and formulation.** The cheapest margin is designed in. What can change without the consumer noticing?
3. **Co-man and MOQs.** Renegotiate from data, not relief.

Step 1 alone usually finds two to five points.

If you want to do this with me, we should talk.
