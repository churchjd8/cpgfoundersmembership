What we learned

The stories land. The ask comes too late.

I went through all 11 sequences, 59 automations and the last 30 broadcasts. Five things stood out.

1
Small, warm groups convert. Event registrants and hand-raisers click 17–58% of the time. The full list clicks 2–5%. That gap is the whole opportunity: the people leaning in should get a different email than everyone else.
2
Your stories are the engine. The end zone, the July 3rd call, Krystal's raise, Kilimanjaro and the honest ledger hold attention better than anything else in the sequence. They stay.
3
The welcome sequence never really asks. Over 31 days, the first link to work with you shows up on Day 25. By then a quarter of people have drifted. And the new $997 Strategy Session isn't mentioned at all.
4
Too many directions. Ten welcome emails point to nine different things: resources, LinkedIn, a poll, Babu, WhatsApp, the MBA, the book, and the application. Each is good. Together, people don't know what you want them to do.
5
After Day 31, founders go quiet. There's no weekly rhythm after the welcome, so a founder who finishes it only hears from you when there's a launch.

Housekeeping I'm cleaning up

  • Paying clients and buyers still receive "apply to work with me" emails. Adding rules so customers stop getting pitched.
  • Two welcome sequences are running the same copy. Merging them into one.
  • Six older sequences (the DreamMakers accelerator, the February masterclass, the April re-engagement, finished Babu flows) are still switched on, some with dead links and retired offers. Turning them off.
Good news: the free-resource delivery is solid. Every kit form tags the founder, sends the kit, and starts the welcome sequence, and every download link works.

The rebuild

Three pieces, one job: more conversations.

Goal: 2 to 5 sales conversations a week from the list, without becoming the guy who emails too much.

Free resourceKits, masterclass, WhatsApp
Already working. Delivers the kit and hands the founder to the welcome sequence.
▼
Welcome v310 emails · 21 days
Your best stories, rebuilt around coach Taki Moore's five email types: Problem, Proof, Philosophy, Plan, Ask. Each ends with "we should talk." The Strategy Session shows up on Day 5 instead of never. Day 1 asks founders to reply with their stage, so we know who's who.
▼
The Tuesday LetterWeekly · everyone
One idea a week, pulled from your best LinkedIn post, plus a Founder Mailbag: a real question from a founder and your straight answer. Turns the great replies we're getting into content, and shows people you actually read them.
▼
The Hot ListEvery other week · engaged only
Anyone who clicks a "work with Jeff" link, replies, joins an event, or starts Babu gets tagged automatically for 30 days. They get one short, blunt email from you every two weeks that ends in a direct invitation to talk.
How much email is that?

About 4 a month for most founders. About 6 a month for the founders who are actively leaning in. Launches like Hot Seat stay as their own short pushes, 3 to 4 a year.

How we'll know it works

A weekly scoreboard: new subscribers, replies, Hot List size, Strategy Sessions sold, applications. Every offer link gets tracked so we can see which email sold.
