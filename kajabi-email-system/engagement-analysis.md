# Engagement analysis (Kajabi data as of 2026-10-28)

## Read this first: what the numbers can and can't tell us

- **Opens are inflated.** Apple Mail Privacy Protection "opens" every email for a big share of readers. A 55% open rate on Jeff's list is mostly noise. Use opens only to compare emails sent to the same audience, never as proof anyone read something.
- **Clicks are the real signal in Kajabi.** But for a high-ticket offer, the money comes from conversations, and the best emails here ask for replies, not clicks.
- **Replies aren't tracked anywhere.** Kajabi doesn't count them, and they land in Jeff's inbox. We don't know how many people answered the topic poll (v2 Email 3) or "what are you working on?" (Existing Email 4). That's the single biggest blind spot. Fix: see "Measurement" in `strategy.md`.
- **Applications aren't attributed.** /apply doesn't record which email sent the person. Same fix.

## The list

About 1,545 contacts on a full-list send (Hot Seat launch, Oct 15). Broadcast benchmarks across the last 30 broadcasts:

| Send type | Typical open | Typical click |
|---|---|---|
| Full-list announcement (Hot Seat, CPG Match, Fatal Flaws promo, WhatsApp invite) | 46-57% | 2-5% |
| Registrant / hand-raiser follow-up (workshop recaps, "ff retarget", Fatal Flaws live) | 65-79% | 17-34% |

The gap is the whole story: **small, warm segments click 5 to 10 times more than the full list.** The "ff retarget" broadcast went to 19 people and got 32% clicks. The Burn Rate workshop follow-up went to 118 and got 28%. That's the case for the Hot List.

What stands out:

1. **The ask comes too late and too rarely.** Over 31 days there are two links to /apply, both in the last week, by which point a quarter of people have dropped out. In Taki Moore's terms, the sequence is all Problem and Story with almost no "we should talk."
2. **The $997 Strategy Session doesn't exist in the sequence.** It's now the front door to working with Jeff (buy now, credited toward advisory within 30 days), and it's the easiest yes on the ladder. The sequence still sells "apply, 2 or 3 spots."
3. **Too many different asks.** Ten emails point at: resources, LinkedIn, a poll, Babu, WhatsApp, MBA, the book, /apply, nothing, /apply + three alternatives. Each is fine. Together nobody knows what Jeff wants them to do.
4. **Attention falls off a cliff at Day 15.** Opens go 62% → 50% between Email 4 and 5 and stay there. Compress the important material into the first 14 days.
5. **Stories carry it.** Emails 2, 8 and 9 (end zone, Krystal, Kilimanjaro/rehab) are the voice of the brand. Keep them.
6. **Unsubscribes are healthy** (0-1% per email). The list tolerates more direct asks than it's getting.

## What's driving applications today

Mostly not email. Kajabi shows ~17 total clicks to /apply from the welcome sequence's Close and a similar handful from Email 8. Broadcasts for events (Hot Seat, Fatal Flaws webinar) get registrations, and the replay email with "what to do next" got 10% clicks. LinkedIn DMs and the WhatsApp group are doing the heavy lifting on calls.

Email's job in the new system: **turn the 1,500-person list into a steady 2 to 5 sales conversations a week** (applications, Strategy Session buys, or "yes, let's talk" replies).
