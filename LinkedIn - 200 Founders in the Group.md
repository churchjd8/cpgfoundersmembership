## Post

We just crossed 200 founders in the CPG Founders WhatsApp group.

When I started it I figured maybe a few dozen people would want a place to ask the questions you can't really ask anywhere else. The ones you don't want to put in front of your board, or your investors, or your own team.

Turns out a lot of us have those questions.

Here is what came through in that group in the last week alone.

One founder walked everybody through an AI inventory management system they built themselves. Screen recording, the whole thing, just handed it over.

Another one needed a military broker and had a warm intro within a few hours.

Somebody shared the merchandising app they actually use every day, not the one with the best sales deck.

And one founder got connected to a pre-seed angel group.

That is one week, nobody got paid for any of it, and nobody was selling anything.

This is the part I love most at this stage of my career. I spent a lot of years learning things the hard way and paying full tuition for it, so watching founders cut each other's learning curves down in a group chat is about as good as it gets.

If you are building in CPG and you are not in there yet, come join us. It is free and it stays free.

https://cpgfoundersgroup.com/founders-only

Come say hi when you get in, tell us what you are building and where you are stuck right now. Somebody in there has already been through it!
