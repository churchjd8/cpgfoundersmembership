---

Hi {{first_name}},

I asked a question in our CPG founders WhatsApp group a couple of Saturdays ago.

We're working on where Proda goes next, and I wanted to know who has the better velocities, GNC or Vitamin Shoppe. If you could only get into one of them, which one makes the most sense?

I've been doing this for 40 years and I didn't know the answer.

It was 9 o'clock on a Saturday morning. Within 40 minutes I had my first reply, and by lunch I had four.

One founder gave me a pick straight away. Another told me which one they'd rather shop in as a customer. A third offered to dig up the category data from when their brand was in one of those programs. And a fourth told me which of the two is harder to do business with, and what to look for in the contract before I sign anything (payment terms, chargebacks, the stuff you usually only learn after it has cost you money).

By the end of the day somebody had sent over notes from a co-founder who had been on that shelf with a previous brand.

I couldn't have bought that from a consultant, and I certainly wasn't going to get it from the buyer!

When I started this group I figured I'd be the one answering the questions. Turns out I'm in there asking them like everybody else.

There are 350 CPG founders in there now, every stage from pre-launch to exit, and nobody is selling anything (that's the one rule). It's free and it stays free.

If you're building in CPG and you're not in there yet, come join us.

[Join the CPG Founders Group](https://cpgfoundersgroup.com/founders-only)

Bring the question you've been stuck on. Chances are somebody in there has already been through it!

Jeff
