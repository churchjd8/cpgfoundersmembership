## 1. The big decisions (my recommendations)

| Decision | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Live or recorded | **Recorded, watch instantly.** No fake "live," no fake countdown to a start time. | Ad traffic is impatient. Instant access gets 2x the watch rate of "come back Thursday." And pretending it's live is off-brand for Jeff. |
| One webinar or two | **One webinar, two ad angles.** Segment by the stage dropdown at registration. | Both audiences need the same five lessons. One recording for Jeff, two doors in. |
| Length | **40 min teaching + 8 min offer.** | Long enough to earn a $497 ask, short enough for cold traffic to finish. |
| Price | **$497 for 3 days after registering (closes midnight Pacific on day 3)** (list stays $997). Klarna stays on. | New and pre-launch founders are the most price-sensitive people Jeff sells to. Half off is easy to say and easy to believe because the $997 page is real and public. |
| Deadline | **Real one.** After 72 hours the offer page shows $997. | A deadline that doesn't hold is a lie, and Jeff's audience talks to each other in a 300-person WhatsApp group. |
| Guarantee | **30-day money back.** | The page has none today. Cold traffic buying a $497 course from someone they met 45 minutes ago needs it. Refund rates on real courses run low single digits. |
| Test order | **Warm audience first, ads second.** | Email list + WhatsApp + LinkedIn cost nothing and tell us in one week whether the webinar converts. Don't pay Meta to find out the pitch is off. |

### The pushback

The front end of this funnel will probably break even, not print money. Rough math on cold traffic (assumptions, not promises):

| | Conservative | Decent | Good |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost per registration | $20 | $15 | $10 |
| Registrants who buy | 1.5% | 3% | 4% |
| Revenue per registrant at $497 | $7.45 | $14.91 | $19.88 |
| Front-end result | lose $12.55/lead | break even | make $9.88/lead |

So the real profit is behind the course: Babu subscriptions ($39 to $199/mo), the 3-Month Intensive ($7,500), the book. That means two things have to be built in from day one:

1. **A next step on the welcome page** (Babu trial for every buyer, Intensive application for anyone already selling).
2. **Every non-buyer drops into the existing nurture sequence**, not a dead end. A $15 lead that buys Babu in month two still paid for itself.

If Jeff wants the front end to be clearly profitable on its own, the lever is price ($697 instead of $497), not more ad spend. I'd start at $497 to get data fast, then test up.

## 2. Who we're talking to

### Audience A — "Just launched, and it's a different beast"
In market 0 to 24 months. Under $500K. First retailer or first few. Came from another career and it shows: confused by distributors, chargebacks, slotting, trade spend, brokers. Cash is tighter than the plan said. Scared they're making an expensive mistake right now and can't see it.

- **What they say:** "Nobody told me about deductions." "I got into 200 stores and I'm losing money." "Do I need a broker?"
- **Hook:** You're already in it. Here's what's coming in the next 12 months and what it costs if you find out the hard way.
- **Jeff's story for them:** "What's a cat man?" / the $1M spoilage bill / Rowdy's $1M slotting fee, gone in 8 months.

### Audience B — "About to launch, want to get it right"
Has a product or a recipe, maybe a brand name, maybe a co-man quote. Pre-revenue. Reading everything. Afraid of wasting their savings.

- **What they say:** "How much does it really cost?" "Where do I even start?" "Should I raise money first?"
- **Hook:** It's $150K to $350K and 9 to 12 months to get on shelf. Here's the order to do it in so you don't pay twice.
- **Jeff's story for them:** "What's a HACCP plan?" (six companies and a Harvard MBA in, and he still didn't know) / the 8-phase Pre-Launch Roadmap.

### Who this is NOT for
Brands past $1M to $2M. They should be sent to the Intensive, and the registration page stage dropdown lets us route them there in email 1 instead of pitching a $497 course.

## 3. The training

**Title:** The First 24 Months of Your CPG Brand (Joshua's pick, Oct 12)
**Subtitle:** 5 things every new food and beverage founder has to get right, and what each one costs when you don't.

Why this title: it's the exact phrase Joshua used to describe the audience, it works for both pre-launch and just-launched, and it sets up the offer without naming it. If the industry is a different beast, you need a different education.

The five lessons, each one a door into an MBA module:

| # | Lesson | Jeff's proof | MBA module it opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The real price of admission.** $150K to $350K, 9 to 12 months, and the Rule of Twos. | HACCP story. 8-phase roadmap. | 01 Industry & Fatal Flaws |
| 2 | **Your margin is decided before you sell a unit.** Shelf price minus retailer, distributor, trade spend, COGS. 40% by end of year 2, 50% by year 3 to 4. | Kombucha at 12% vs wellness shots at 60%. One swap took Suja from 32% to 40%. | 03 Revenue, Expenses & Financial Modeling |
| 3 | **Stores are not the goal. Velocity is.** Shelf space is rented every week. You get about 9 months to prove you belong. | Rowdy paid Albertsons ~$1M in slotting and was discontinued in 8 months. Billboards in Manhattan with four stores. | 05 Sales, 06 Scaling |
| 4 | **Cash is oxygen, and equity is the most expensive money you'll ever spend.** 18 months of runway, 15 to 20% dilution per round. | The $200M exit where the founder owns 4%. 44 rounds of raising. | 02 Fundraising, 07 Ops & Cash |
| 5 | **The paper you sign can end you.** Personal guarantees, evergreen contracts, who owns your formula. | $1M spoilage bill from a handshake. "Trust must be documented." | 01 Fatal Flaws |

Full slide-by-slide and talk track are in the script file.

## 7. Traffic

### Phase 1 — warm, free (week one after the page is live)
- Email to the full Kajabi list.
- Founders Club WhatsApp note.
- 2 LinkedIn posts from Jeff (one per audience).
- Add the training as a lead magnet on `/resources`.
- **The Cold Pressed Truth launches Nov 17.** The book's toolbox page and launch emails should point to this training. Book readers are the best-qualified traffic this funnel will ever see.

Target: 150+ registrations. That's enough to see watch rate, click rate to the offer, and sales before spending a dollar.

### Phase 2 — Meta ads
Budget to test: about $1,500 over 14 days ($100/day), 2 angles x 3 creatives.

**Angle A (just launched)**
- "I ran six companies and had a Harvard MBA. Then a Whole Foods buyer asked me for my HACCP plan and I had no idea what she was talking about."
- "You got into 200 stores. Congratulations. Now you have about 9 months to prove you belong there."
- "A $5.99 shelf price is about $2 to you. Here's where the rest goes."

**Angle B (pre-launch)**
- "It costs $150K to $350K to get a food or beverage brand on shelf. Most founders budget half that."
- "Thinking about launching a food or drink brand? Do these 8 things in this order."
- "The most expensive way to learn CPG is by doing it."

**Creative:** Jeff to camera, 30 to 60 seconds, phone quality is fine. Record all six in the same session as the webinar. Plus two static images (the price waterfall slide, the 8-phase roadmap slide).

**Targeting:** broad US, 25 to 55, let the creative do the targeting. Layer interest tests (Expo West, BevNET, Whole Foods supplier, Shopify food and beverage). Retarget page visitors and 50% video viewers with the testimonial cut.

**Kill or scale after 14 days:**
- Registration page converts under 20% of clicks: fix the page before touching ads.
- Cost per registration over $25: creative problem.
- Under 30% of registrants start the video: fix email 1 and 2.
- Under 1.5% of registrants buy: fix the pitch or the offer, don't scale.
- 3%+ buying at $15 or less per registration: scale and test $697.
