Had the intro call with Mayssa, founder of BEHAVE Candy, on Monday. She sent the data room after. Deck, P&L summary, unit economics by channel, and a retail velocity report. Attaching all four.

Short version: I like this one a lot. Real brand, real product differentiation, real retail traction, and a founder who is clear-eyed and easy to talk to. There are also three or four things in the numbers that I want us to pressure test before we get excited about a check.

## The business

Low sugar, clean label gummy candy. 1g to 3g sugar per pouch, 12g fiber, no sugar alcohols and no artificial sweeteners, which is the actual moat versus SmartSweets. Founder is ex NFL, ex Uber, then early Daily Harvest through the 10M to 100M run rate year. Co-founder Emily Gu was first ops hire at Native through the P&G acquisition. Chef Elizabeth Falkner leads R&D. Busy Philipps and Norman Powell are investors, Magic Spoon founders are advisors and investors, Away founder is in. $4.5M raised to date, board is just the two founders.

They launched in 2020, got wrecked by COVID supply chain, spent three years rebuilding, relaunched end of 2023. Went viral on TikTok through 2024. Now nationwide at Target with 3 SKUs in ~2,000 doors, did a Sam's Club test in Q1, and Costco and BJ's both land in September.

## The numbers

Net revenue: $478K (2024), $1.6M (2025), $3.4M (2026). Gross margin 45% in 2025, 37% in 2026. EBITDA has been roughly flat around negative $250K to $315K each year, so they are not lighting money on fire.

The forward model is where it gets aggressive. 2027 at $6.4M, 2028 at $17.8M, 2029 at $57.5M. That is 17x off today in three years, and the growth rate accelerates rather than decelerates (90%, then 177%, then 222%). They also model 2028 as the first profitable year at 4% EBITDA and 2029 at 18%.

## What I want us to dig into

1. **The Target velocity trend.** This is my biggest one. Their own sell-through chart shows weekly units peaking around 19K in mid May and drifting down to about 10K by end of July. Every peak week is a promoted week. Unpromoted base is sitting at 10K to 12K and softening. Chainwide velocity is 2.26 units per SKU per store per week. Top 100 doors do 4.91, which is great, but the chain average is what the buyer looks at going into line review. Jeff, you know the real threshold at Target candy better than anyone. Is 2.26 survivable into year two or is that a cut?

2. **DTC has been shrinking for three years.** $73K, then $70K, then $65K. The 2027 model has it jumping to $932K, a 14x. The entire case rests on the new GLP-1 fiber angle they started testing two weeks ago. She quoted CAC of $10 to $15 and a test where $30 in spend produced five purchases. I believe the angle is real, it is working everywhere in the space right now, but that is a very small sample carrying a very large number in the model.

3. **TikTok Shop revenue fell 72%.** $531K in 2025 down to $149K in 2026. The deck leads with 29M reach and 100% organic virality, and she framed the drop as a deliberate pivot to drive people into stores. Plausible. But the model has TTS going back to $633K in 2027 and $6.3M by 2029, and I want to know what brings it back.

4. **Concentration.** Retail is 89% of 2026 revenue and effectively all of that is Target. One buyer decision moves the entire business.

5. **Club economics.** Their unit econ sheet has two club columns with very different profiles. One shows 35% contribution margin, the other 20%. Need to know which retailer maps to which, and whether the September Costco and BJ's rotations are cash positive after MCBs and demos. Club rotations at 20% CM are a working capital trap, and a $2M to $3M raise funding a Costco rotation plus a paid media ramp plus inventory could be thin. Worth asking whether the round is sized right.

6. **Margin direction.** Gross margin went from 45% to 37% as they shifted into retail, and the model has it climbing back to 43% by 2029 while club (their lowest margin channel) becomes a bigger share. That needs an explanation.

7. **Pre 2024 P&L.** They raised $4.5M but the summary only starts at 2024, where cumulative losses are about $1.4M. Want to see 2020 through 2023 to understand where the rest went and whether there is debt outstanding. There is a non operating expense line running $22K to $370K a year that looks like interest.

## Valuation

Not in the data room. No ask slide, no cap table, no use of funds. On the call she said $2M to $3M, seed plus, terms set by the lead, open to a priced round or a note. Some investors she is talking to want a note to move fast, others will only lead priced.

For framing, and this is our estimate and not hers, a brand at $3.4M with Target national and three club accounts landing would typically get priced somewhere in the high teens to high twenties post. If nobody has anchored her yet, there is room for us to shape the terms rather than react to them.
