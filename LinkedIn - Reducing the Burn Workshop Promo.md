"35 cents for your can and label?!"

I looked up in disbelief. One of my clients was being completely overcharged on his can supply and had no idea. Nobody had ever told him what he should be paying... he just accepted the number his supplier gave him and moved on.

That one line item alone was eating his margins alive.

This is what I see constantly with first-time CPG founders. It's not that they're making bad decisions — it's that nobody ever gave them the benchmarks. What healthy margins actually look like at their stage. How much trade spend is appropriate (and when). Which promotions are moving product and which ones are just burning cash.

If nobody's told you what to measure, how are you supposed to fix it?

After working with hundreds of founders I can usually tell within the first conversation where the money is going. The answers are almost always hiding in the same places — input costs that were never questioned, trade spend that isn't being tracked, a product mix with one or two SKUs dragging everything down.
