**Bio:**
Jeff Church is a serial entrepreneur, operator and investor with more than 30 years of experience building consumer brands. He is the co-founder and former CEO of Suja Juice, which grew from zero to $100 million in revenue in six years and became one of the fastest-growing food and beverage companies in America, with Coca-Cola as a strategic partner and investor. A Harvard Business School graduate and former CPA, Jeff has raised more than $212 million and returned nearly $700 million to investors across eight ventures: five home runs and three strikeouts.

Today Jeff is the founder of CPG Founders Group, where he mentors the next generation of consumer brand founders through one-on-one advising, free tools and training, the Founders Club community of more than 350 founders, and askBabu.ai, an AI advisor built on his playbooks. He is also building his ninth company, Proda, a protein soda brand.

His book, The Cold Pressed Truth: A Founder's Field Guide to Building, Scaling, and Selling a Consumer Brand, with a foreword by Jay Shetty, comes out November 17, 2026. It is part memoir and part operating manual, covering everything from testing an idea and raising capital to winning shelf space, protecting cash and navigating an exit.

Outside of work, Jeff has run more than 55 marathons and ultramarathons and attempted six of the Seven Summits, reaching five. He lives in San Diego with his wife, Linda, and is a father of four and grandfather of three. His grandkids call him Babu, Swahili for grandpa.
