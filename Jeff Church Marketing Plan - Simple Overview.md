1. **Build the machine** — set up systems that capture leads, nurture them with real value, and guide them toward your paid offers over time.

The goal is simple: grow your email list, stay top of mind, and convert the right founders into paying clients. Everything below supports that.

## The Ecosystem (How It All Connects)

Every channel points to one place: **your email list.**

Once someone is on your list, the emails do the work. You don't have to sell on LinkedIn or on a podcast. You just have to get them to download something free. The system handles the rest.

---

## The Channels (What We're Doing)

### 1. Email Marketing

- **Nurture sequence (automated):** When someone signs up for any free resource, they get 9 emails over 30 days. Starts with pure value, introduces Babu, then MBA for CPG, then 90-Day Breakthrough. Ends with a clear "here's how we work together" email.
- **Weekly newsletter (new):** Once someone finishes the nurture sequence, they get a weekly email from you — one lesson or story, plus links to your latest blog post and LinkedIn content. Keeps you top of inbox without being pushy.
- **Existing contacts:** Your ~1,000 existing contacts get entered into a separate nurturing sequence so they experience some new value and new touchpoints from you.

### 2. LinkedIn (Organic)

- 2 posts per week — stories and lessons from your experience
- Not polished corporate content. Raw, real, the way you actually talk
- Every post links to a specific free resource so followers become email subscribers
- First 8 weeks: 16 posts already mapped out

### 3. Blog / SEO

- 5 blog posts per week (Monday–Friday)
- Each one targets a specific keyword CPG founders are searching for
- Each one links to a relevant free resource
- Long-term play — these posts compound over time and bring in organic traffic from Google

### 4. LinkedIn Outbound

- Using Sales Navigator and your list of active followers to find ideal founders
- Sending 15-25 personalized messages per day offering free value (a specific tool or white paper — not a sales pitch)
- Goal: get them into the ecosystem, not sell them directly

### 5. Podcast Guesting

- Pitching you as a guest on CPG and founder-focused podcasts
- 3 pitches per day using a proven structure
- Your credibility makes this easy: 8x founder, $700M in exits, Suja/Coca-Cola, the book
- Every appearance ends with one CTA: download a free resource at cpgfoundersgroup.com

### 6. Partnerships

- Reaching out to CPG communities and organizations to offer free workshops
- You teach a session for their members, they promote it, we capture the emails
- 1-2 outreaches per week — smaller scale but high quality leads

### 7. Cold Email (Later)

- Targeted, low-volume outreach to qualified founders
- Only after other channels are running smoothly
- Separate from LinkedIn outbound — no founder gets hit on both channels

### 8. Paid Ads (Testing)

- Small budget test ($500-1K) driving to your best free resource
- Only after we know which resources convert best organically
- If it works, we scale it. If it doesn't, we haven't wasted much.

### 9. The Book

- Separate launch campaign when you publish it.

## How We Measure Success

Two numbers matter most:

1. **Email subscribers** — how many new founders are entering the ecosystem each week?
2. **Paid conversions** — how many are signing up for Babu, MBA, 90-Day, or VIP?

Secondary metrics we'll track: LinkedIn follower growth, blog traffic, podcast appearances booked, email open rates.

We'll do a full review at the end of 8 weeks to see what's working and what to adjust.
