## Post

Something I noticed going back through our CPG founders WhatsApp group...

The question that comes up more than any other isn't about fundraising, or margins, or how to get into Whole Foods. It's "does anybody have a marketing agency they'd actually recommend?"

Right behind it: how do I get my first retail meeting, who did your packaging, which AI tools are you actually using, and what are normal distributor terms.

I find that interesting, because those are exactly the questions where the honest answer never comes from a Google search or a sales deck. It comes from a founder who hired the agency, sat through the six months, and can tell you straight whether it was worth it.

That's what the group has turned into. Somebody asks, and usually by the end of the day three or four founders who have already been through it are weighing in. Nobody is selling anything in there (that's the one rule, and it's why it works).

I spent a lot of years at Suja learning things the expensive way, when a ten minute conversation with the right founder would have gotten me there. So watching 350 founders do that for each other every day is about as good as it gets at this stage of my career.

If you're building in CPG and you're not in there yet, come join us. It's free and it stays free.

cpgfoundersgroup.com/founders-only

Tell us what you're building and what you're stuck on when you get in. Chances are somebody in there has already solved it!
