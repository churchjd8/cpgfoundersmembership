# Membership Restructure Email — Founding Members

**From:** Jeff Church
**To:** CPG Founders Group founding members
**Subject:** A few changes (and a thank you) for our founding members

---

Hi founders,

I wanted to share an update with you all on our work together in the CPG Founders Membership. 

When we launched the membership, The intention was for it to be built largely around group delivery through office hours, email support, and tool access. That was the plan on paper and why we were able to price the membership where we did. But here's what actually happened: the one-on-one calls turned out to be where the real value was. The group stuff sounded great in theory, but in practice, the one-on-ones have been what is really required for me to support you the way I want.

After reaching full capacity with this, I have to restructure slightly to lean into what's actually helping you and what can be sustainable for me. With the launch of Proda on top of everything else, I can't be in fifteen places at half-strength. I'd rather be in fewer places at full strength and show up fully for you.

Here's what that looks like.

**A note on new clients.** Going forward, anyone new I take on will be coming in at $5,000/month, built entirely around one-on-one work and access to my tools. As founding members, you've been with me for a while, and I want to honor that. You will not be paying new-client rates.

**For most of you, this won't change much.** I just have to get tighter around how I structure it. At your $1,500/month rate, here's what you keep:

- One full hour 1:1 with me each month as the centerpiece.
- Email support beyond that. I'm in your corner between calls.
- BabuAI Pro included ($200/month), plus access to all of my models and tools and the MBA for CPG modules.
- Emergency availability when you actually need it. If something's on fire, I won't leave you high and dry.

If you'd like more support than that, I can offer you a discounted rate for a steadier cadence: two 1:1's a month at $3,000/month. That's a founding-member rate, just for the folks who want more of my time on their business. No pressure either way.

**What's going away:** I'm sunsetting the office hours and the accountability calls. Participation has been low, and honestly, when people did show up they turned into one-on-ones anyway. So I'd rather give you that time directly. I'll still bring in speakers and guests from time to time, and when I do, you're all invited no charge to anything I put together.

**One scheduling heads-up.** I take one real break a year, and it's coming up. From roughly mid-July to mid-August I'll be on the East Coast at the lakehouse with my grandkids and family. So for July and August I'll be scheduling calls with you for early July and late August. I'll still be reachable by email the whole time and phone for emergencies. None of you will lose a session. We'll simply schedule your monthly one-on-ones around those weeks.

Last thing. If this new shape doesn't fit what you're looking for, you can step away whenever you need to, no hard feelings. I'd hate to see you go, and I mean that. I genuinely love working with you, and I want to keep supporting you and hope you have been receiving value from our work together. 

If your current tier works as-is, you don't need to do anything, I will reach out and schedule your next 1:1 if not already. If you want to move up to the biweekly spot, or you've got a question about any of this, just hit reply and we'll sort it out.

Thank you for being here and I am grateful to be in your corner. 

Jeff
