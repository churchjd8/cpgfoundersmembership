# Membership Restructure Email — Founding Members

**From:** Jeff Church
**To:** CPG Founders Group founding members
**Subject:** A few changes (and a thank you) for our founding members

---

Hi friends,

I want to be straight with you, because that's how I've tried to run this group from day one.

When we launched the membership, I built it around group delivery and email support. That was the plan on paper. But here's what actually happened: the one-on-one calls and the tools, especially askBabu, turned out to be where the real value was. That's where we moved your business forward. The group stuff sounded great in theory. In practice, the one-on-ones did the work.

So I'm restructuring to lean into what's actually helping you, and to be honest about my capacity. With the launch of Proto on top of everything else, I can't be in fifteen places at half-strength. I'd rather be in fewer places at full strength and genuinely show up for you.

Here's what that looks like.

**A note on new clients.** Going forward, anyone new I take on comes in at $5,000/month, built entirely around one-on-one work. I'm telling you that not to sell you anything, but so you understand what I'm protecting when I say the next part: you're founding members, you've been with me for a while, and I want to honor that. You are not paying new-client rates.

**Your founding member options:**

**1. $1,500/month** (where most of you are today)
- One one-on-one with me each month, anchored around a full hour. That's the centerpiece.
- Email support beyond that. I'm in your corner between calls.
- askBabu Pro included. That's a $200/month tool, on the house as part of your membership.
- Emergency availability when you actually need it. If something's on fire, I'll get on the phone.

**2. $3,000/month** (if you want more of me)
- Everything above, but biweekly. Two standing one-on-ones a month on my calendar.
- This is for the founders who want a steadier cadence and more of my time on their business.

**What's going away:** I'm sunsetting the office hours and the accountability calls. Participation has been low, and honestly, when people did show up they turned into one-on-ones anyway. So I'd rather give you that time directly. I'll still bring in speakers and guests from time to time, and when I do, you're all invited to anything I put together.

**One scheduling heads-up.** I take one real break a year, and this is it. From roughly mid-July to mid-August I'll be on the East Coast at the lake. For about three weeks in there I'll be off calls. I'll still be reachable by email the whole time, and in the couple of weeks leading in I'll be on a lighter call schedule. None of you will lose a session. We'll simply schedule your monthly one-on-ones around those weeks. If anything, plan a little ahead with me and we'll lock in your time.

Last thing. Everything here is month-to-month. It always has been. If this new shape doesn't fit what you're looking for, you can step away whenever you need to, no hard feelings. I'd hate to see you go, and I mean that. I genuinely love working with you, and I want to keep doing it for a long time.

If your current tier works as-is, you don't need to do anything. If you want to move up to the biweekly spot, or you've got a question about any of this, just hit reply and we'll sort it out.

Thank you for being here from the start. The founders who raised their hand early are the ones I'll always go to the wall for.

Jeff
