### Your 5 Free Tools

1. **[Pricing Calculator](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1BzsfbD05XuR6-XCust69_OGjfPqSPcih)** — Start from MSRP and work down, or start from COGS and build up. Compare both to find your optimal price.

2. **[Trade Promo Break-Even Calculator](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1TOA5xBS_4Iwl_ChKbr8O6DT0yVkgKtJv)** — Should you run that promo? Break-even lift, P&L comparison, and a traffic-light score so you know before you commit.

3. **[SKU Rationalization Tool](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1TsyjcooNakJi4ROVMEB0Ta8e-MO4rqGa)** — See which SKUs are carrying your brand and which are dragging it down. Margin thresholds, portfolio contribution, keep/monitor/cut decisions.

4. **[Cash Conversion Cycle Tool](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1eejrhY2elYC-mO5lG4b2GWvmhzEpv3AF)** — See exactly how your payment terms trap or free cash. Change any input and watch the dollar impact in real time.

5. **[Cash Runway Calculator](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1scxRZdGvg1YSDGpY828dT7j_oBrH-0Ck)** — Model your burn rate across 3 years, calculate your exact runway, and figure out how much you need to raise.

These are the same tools I use with my portfolio brands. Download them, plug in your numbers, and you'll have a clearer picture of your business by tomorrow morning.
